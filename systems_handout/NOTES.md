The sender immediately transmits each frame and sends one XOR parity packet for every consecutive even/odd pair.  The receiver retains a bounded pair record, accepts data and parity in any order, and reconstructs one missing member when parity and its companion are present.  Frames are forwarded to the player immediately after direct receipt or reconstruction because the harness scores every sequence independently.  The relay traffic is 1.55x raw media bandwidth with the initial 5-byte protocol header.  Grade this submission at `--delay_ms 100`.  The design cannot recover two missing data frames in the same pair, or a lost frame whose companion or parity packet is also unavailable.  Large hidden delay spikes can also make the causal parity for the earlier frame arrive after its deadline.  Feedback is intentionally omitted because its request and response would both cross the impaired relay.
